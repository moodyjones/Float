The Ritual

Twenty minutes,
start to finish

The balm reaches full activation at twenty minutes of heat. That's not a suggestion — it's the window. Run the glass and follow it.

20:00Ready
  1. Before

    Apply to target areas

    A finger's length across the upper chest before sauna or steam. Any areas that feel inflamed or need attention. Wash hands after use.

  2. 0—15 min

    Cooling begins

    Aromatics open. By ten minutes muscles feel eased and the breath feels clearer.

  3. 15—20 min

    Soothed + conditioned

    At fifteen minutes the skin feels soothed and conditioned. Heat may intensify the sensation.

  4. After

    Post-heat

    Hydrated, softened and reset. The window after matters as much as the window before.
